Claim documents

What you'll need to file a claim

Requirements vary by policy type — here's the short version for the most common categories.

Motor

FIR copy (theft or major accident), policy document, RC copy, driving licence, photographs of the damage, repair estimate from a network garage, and the filled claim form.

Health

Hospital discharge summary, original bills, prescriptions, diagnostic reports, ID proof, policy copy, KYC documents, plus pre-authorization for cashless claims.

Fire

Fire brigade report, FIR copy, photographs of the damage, stock register or inventory, books of accounts, repair or replacement quotations, claim form and policy schedule.

Marine

Bill of lading or LR copy, invoice and packing list, survey report, photographs, claim bill, joint survey report where applicable, and correspondence with the carrier.

Term life

Death certificate, claim form, policy bond, nominee ID proof, hospital records where applicable, post-mortem report for accidental death, and FIR copy.

Cyber

Forensic report, FIR or cybercrime complaint, evidence of the breach, financial loss documentation, any regulatory notices, and customer notification records.

FAQ

Claim questions, answered

How quickly should I report a claim?
As soon as you can — ideally within 24 hours for motor and fire claims, and within 48 hours for health claims, or as your policy wording requires. Delayed reporting is the single most common cause of claim disputes.
What is a cashless claim?
In a cashless claim the insurer settles the bill directly with the hospital or garage, so you don't pay upfront except for excluded items. For planned hospitalizations we usually get pre-authorization within 2–4 hours.
What if my claim is rejected?
Most rejections come from documentation gaps, late reporting or a genuine policy exclusion. We review the rejection letter, identify the cause, and where the rejection is contestable we file a representation or escalate to the IRDAI Ombudsman.
How long does claim settlement take?
Motor claims typically 7–15 days. Health cashless approval 2–6 hours; health reimbursement 15–30 days. Fire and marine claims 30–90 days depending on the surveyor's report. We follow up at every stage.
Do I lose my No Claim Bonus after a claim?
For motor insurance, usually yes — a claim resets your NCB at renewal. An NCB Protection add-on lets you make a limited number of claims without losing the bonus; we'll tell you whether the extra premium is worth it in your case.
